I.R.Choir - Place Presets

55 original algorithmic designs covering all 55 entries in the public
Spaces II Locations list, across 18 categories. These are creative reference
profiles, not measured acoustics, EastWest impulse responses or true-stereo
recordings. There is no affiliation or endorsement. Their 1,020 individual
recorded presets are not reproduced. Gated Room is a short-room design;
it does not add a separate gating processor.

Open Reverb space, then choose a place in its bottom-left browser.
Each choice updates room type, pre-delay, decay shape, wet width, damping,
low/high cut frequencies and wet level. It enables reverb and both filters,
turns mono send/freeze/wet-only off, and leaves the choir, delay, EQ and
conductor intact. Decay shape is relative, not a measured time in seconds.
The browser shows (edited) after you change its settings. Sound presets and
DAW state retain the place label and the actual parameters. Undo/Redo covers
the whole place selection as one transaction.
